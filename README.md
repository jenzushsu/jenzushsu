# Hi, I'm Jenzus Hsu 👋

I'm a Singapore-based **AI & Developer Platform Solution Architect** at Zoom. I turn ambitious ideas into systems people can use—moving from customer discovery and rapid prototypes to architecture, production delivery, demos, writing, and teaching.

My work sits at the intersection of:

- **Solution architecture** — translating business goals, user workflows, platform capabilities, and technical constraints into executable designs
- **Rapid prototyping** — making the hardest integration real early enough to change the decision
- **Production delivery** — designing for identity, failure modes, observability, and the team that inherits the system
